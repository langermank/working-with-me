# Working with me

This guide highlights a few things about me, and my preferences when it comes to collaborating at work. 

## Personal details

- I'm based in San Diego with my partner and cat 🐈‍⬛
- Originally from Maine and lived in Boston for 10 years
- I watch a lot of movies and TV series
- I'm learning interior design and plotting an unreasonable amount of renovation projects for my home

## Working hours

- I do my best to keep working hours between 9AM - 5PM PST Mon/Tues and Thurs/Fri
- I keep my Wednesday mornings open for early calls
- Morning is my workout time, so if you book a call with me I'll probably be in gym clothes

## Role and experience

- **Currently:** Staff Systems Designer at GitHub
- I have a background in graphic design and visual merchandising
- I've mostly held front-end eng roles, but I feel much more at home on a design team
- I ❤️ CSS

## Communication

- I aim to be responsive and I tend to prioritize getting back to people quickly
- If you don't ping me about it, I probably won't find it elsewhere
- I value 1-1 calls, but I do find them draining and I _will_ need time to recover

## Receiving feedback

- I love feedback and I'm very open to receiving it
- It's easier for me to receive negative feedback if you share it kindly and in a considerate way
- If you are in a leadership role, consider that your words hold a lot of weight and can leave a lasting impact
- I don't love open ended design feedback that doesn't feel actionable: some examples
  - "Make it pop"
  - "X company does it this way, so.."
  - Sharing some analogy that doesn't practically relate to interface design

## Giving feedback

- Sometimes I struggle to share feedback on the spot, I prefer to collect my thoughts and share later
- I find large groups intimidating and prefer giving feedback 1-1 or in a small group
- I try to always give positive feedback before negative feedback
